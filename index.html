About FriendHub

FriendHub is being created as a place where people can discover new friendships and communities based on shared interests.

The goal is to make discovering people and groups easier by bringing different types of social connections together in one place.

FriendHub is an independent platform and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Snapchat, WhatsApp, or Telegram.

Safety matters.
FriendHub is being designed with reporting, blocking, moderation and age-appropriate experiences in mind.
