About FriendHub

A simple place to discover people and communities.

What is FriendHub?

FriendHub is a social discovery platform designed to help people discover friendships, communities and connections based on shared interests.

Users can create posts, discover people and find WhatsApp, Telegram and Snapchat connections.

What you can do

  • Create a personal profile.
  • Discover people with similar interests.
  • Find WhatsApp communities.
  • Find Telegram communities.
  • Share Snapchat-related posts.
  • Create posts and interact with the community.

Important

FriendHub is an independent platform and is not affiliated with Snapchat, WhatsApp or Telegram.

FriendHub is designed with safety, privacy and age-appropriate interactions in mind.
