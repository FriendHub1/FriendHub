About FriendHub

A place to discover people and communities with similar interests.

What is FriendHub?

FriendHub is a social discovery platform designed to help people find new friends and communities based on shared interests, languages and what they are looking for.

Users will be able to create profiles, share posts, discover people and find communities connected through platforms such as Snapchat, WhatsApp and Telegram.

FriendHub is an independent platform and is not affiliated with Snapchat, WhatsApp or Telegram.
